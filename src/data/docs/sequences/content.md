# Sequences

Sequences allow you to group frequently repeated steps into reusable blocks. This simplifies scenario management and ensures consistency across your testing projects.

### Creating a Sequence
1. In your scenario, select several consecutive steps you wish to group.
2. Open the **Actions** menu and select **Create sequence**.
<img src="/images/screenshots/create-sequence.png" alt="Create sequence" width="300" />
3. To rename it, click on the default name and edit the text.
<img src="/images/screenshots/rename-sequence.png" alt="Rename sequence" width="300" />

### Using Sequences
**To add to a scenario:** Click the **Plus (+)** icon, select **Add sequence**, choose your sequence, and click **Add**.
<img src="/images/screenshots/add-seq.png" alt="Add sequence" width="300" />
<img src="/images/screenshots/select-seq.png" alt="Select sequence" width="300" />

**To start a new scenario with a sequence:** Select **Add sequence** on the initial creation screen before recording.
<img src="/images/screenshots/start-from-seq.png" alt="Start from sequence" width="300" />

### Ungrouping and removing sequences

<img src="/images/screenshots/ungroup-remove-seq.png" alt="Ungroup remove sequence" width="300" />

To break a sequence back into individual steps:
1. Right-click the sequence in the scenario editor.
2. Select **Ungroup**.

To remove a sequence from a scenario:
1. Right-click the sequence in the scenario editor.
2. Select **Remove**.
